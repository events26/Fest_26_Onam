⚽ 5-a-Side Football — Official Rulebook

CAFS Onotsavam 2026 · 10 Aug 2026 · Calpanthers Turf, Avanamcode · Gents
🏅 POINTS · 10 · 6 · 4

1. Tournament Format ടൂർണമെന്റ് ഘടന

  • Format: Knockout.
  • Fixtures: Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, Final and Losers' Final (3rd Place Match).
  • Match duration: 20 minutes — 10 minutes per half, with a 2-minute half-time interval.
  • If the scores are level at full time, the winner will be decided by a penalty shootout.

2. Team Composition ടീം ഘടന

  • Each team may register a squad of up to 8 players.
  • Five (5) players, including the goalkeeper, shall be on the field at any one time.
  • Unlimited rolling substitutions are permitted throughout the match.
  • Each team's Managers shall be its existing Team Captains and will serve as the team's official representatives throughout the tournament.
  • All substitutions must take place from the designated substitution area, after the outgoing player has left the field.

3. Match Rules മത്സര നിയമങ്ങൾ

  • All matches shall be played barefoot.
  • Kick-ins shall be used instead of throw-ins.
  • There shall be no offside rule.
  • Goalkeepers may handle the ball only inside their penalty area.
  • Opponents must remain at least 3 metres away during kick-ins and free kicks.
  • Dangerous tackles, tripping, pushing, holding, charging and deliberate handball will be penalized.
  • Yellow and red cards may be issued for misconduct to players, substitutes and team managers.
  • A player receiving a red card shall take no further part in that match.
FORFEIT If two or more members of the same team (playing members, substitute players and/or team managers) receive red cards during a match, that team shall forfeit the match and the opposing team shall be declared the winner.

4. Penalty Shootout പെനാൽറ്റി ഷൂട്ടൗട്ട്

3 penalty kicks per team → still tied? → sudden death
  • Three penalty kicks per team.
  • If still tied, sudden-death penalties will determine the winner.
  • Only players on the field at the end of normal time are eligible to participate.

5. General പൊതു നിർദ്ദേശങ്ങൾ

  • Teams should report at least 10 minutes before the scheduled kick-off.
  • Fair play, discipline and respect towards opponents, officials and fellow employees are expected at all times.
  • The referee's decision on all matters shall be final and binding.

6. In short ചുരുക്കത്തിൽ

Squad of 8, 5 on the pitch, barefoot, no offside, 10 minutes each way. Level at full time goes to penalties. Two red cards in one team and the match is forfeited — so keep it clean. ⚽
