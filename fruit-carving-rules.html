🍉 Fruit Carving Competition — Official Rulebook

CAFS Onotsavam 2026 · 07 Aug 2026 · CAFS Indoor · 1 Gent + 1 Lady per team
🏅 POINTS · 5 · 3 · 2

1. Competition Format മത്സര ഘടന

  • No theme is prescribed. Each team is free to create any original fruit and vegetable carving design.
  • Each participating team shall consist of one gentleman and one lady.
  • Time allowed: 90 minutes.
  • One 4-blade carving tool set will be provided to each team.
  • All carving must be completed within the allotted time.
Materials provided: Watermelon · Pumpkin · Carrot · Beetroot · Leafy Vegetables

Additional fruits or vegetables may be provided at the organizers' discretion.

2. Competition Rules മത്സര നിയമങ്ങൾ

  1. Participants must begin only after the official start signal.
  2. Only the blade set provided by the organizers may be used during the competition.
  3. No pre-carved or pre-prepared decorative items are permitted.
  4. Teams are expected to maintain a clean and safe workstation throughout the event.
  5. The finished carving must be completed within the allotted time.
  6. The judges' decision will be final and binding.

3. Judging Criteria വിധി നിർണയ മാനദണ്ഡങ്ങൾ

Judges will evaluate each team's work based on the following aspects:

AspectWhat the judges look for
Creativity & OriginalityOriginal concepts, imagination, and uniqueness of the design.
Technical SkillPrecision of cuts, symmetry, detailing, control, and carving technique.
Overall AppearanceVisual appeal, balance, elegance, and first impression.
Neatness & FinishClean execution, smooth finish, and professional workmanship.
Use of Fruit & VegetablesEffective use of the provided materials with minimal wastage and good use of natural colours and shapes.
ComplexityDegree of difficulty, depth of carving, and intricacy of the work.
Presentation & StabilityAttractive display, structural stability, and suitability for professional hospitality presentation.

4. In short ചുരുക്കത്തിൽ

One gent and one lady, 90 minutes, only the provided blades and produce — nothing pre-carved. Carve something original, clean and stable, and the judges' word is final. 🏆
