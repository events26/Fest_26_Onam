📐 How the score works from the official game instructions

Every participant gets 5 potatoes — one per round — and must halve each one in a single cut. Both halves are weighed; the closer they are, the higher the accuracy.

Error % = |Half 1 − Half 2| ÷ Whole Potato × 100  →  Accuracy % = 100 − Error %

Final score = average of the 3 highest accuracy scores out of 5. (അഞ്ചിൽ ഏറ്റവും മികച്ച മൂന്ന് സ്കോറുകളുടെ ശരാശരി)
Until 3 rounds are done the board shows the average of whatever has been scored so far — which is why the ranking can move dramatically in Rounds 3, 4 and 5 as weak cuts get dropped.

If two participants tie:

  1. Higher 4th-best accuracy score wins.
  2. Still level → higher average of all 5 scores.
  3. Still level → one sudden-death potato.
⚠️ ONE CUT ONLY — no trimming, shaving, breaking, removing or adding any portion after the cut.
